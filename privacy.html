Vibe PCB Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026. Vibe PCB is developed by Operation Embedded / Anthony Peytchev. Privacy contact: Contact@operationembedded.net.

Local projects and recovery

In on-device mode, projects, board and schematic edits, chat, imported libraries and recovery copies stay in the app’s storage on your device. Vibe PCB does not upload this workspace to a developer-operated server. Device backups managed by Apple may include app data, according to your device settings. Automatic iCloud workspace sync is not enabled. Recovery copies are stored locally, with the previous save plus up to 12 checkpoints and a 60 MB checkpoint retention budget. Deleting the app removes its local storage. Export a backup to Files for an independent copy. Files you export are controlled by the storage provider you choose.

Optional online AI

Online AI is optional and its charges are separate from the app purchase. Before sharing with an online connection, the app asks for consent. Your prompt, board context, recent conversation and attachments you select are sent directly to the AI provider and endpoint you choose. Supported connections include OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI and a custom compatible endpoint. The provider receives request metadata, including your IP address, and handles data under its own terms and retention policies. Provider response and source references are saved in your local chat. Do not send confidential material unless your provider arrangement permits it.

API keys are kept in the device Keychain and are not included in workspace backups or exported projects. Disconnect a provider in Workspace settings to remove its saved key. You can withdraw AI-sharing consent in settings. Apple Basic AI provides limited on-device help on compatible devices without sending requests to an online AI provider through Vibe PCB.

Web research, PDFs and component libraries

When enabled, research sends public search terms to DuckDuckGo and downloads public webpages and text-based PDFs from their publishers. Public part codes and package search terms may be sent to JLCPCB/LCSC, EasyEDA or KiCad/GitHub services to find components and footprints. These services receive network metadata such as your IP address. Retrieved text excerpts and downloaded package geometry may be included in requests to your selected AI. The app retains citations, retrieval notices and imported library data locally. Turn off “Web, PDF & component libraries” in AI settings to disable AI research. Manual inventory/library requests still contact the source you choose.

Optional private host

If you explicitly connect a Mac/private host, that host stores and processes its own workspace and may forward AI requests using its configured provider. Its operator controls access, retention and deletion. This policy does not cover an independently operated host’s practices.

Retention, deletion and support

Projects and messages remain until you remove them or delete app data. Deleted material may remain in local recovery copies until they expire or you remove those copies in settings. Exported files and system backups must be deleted from their respective locations separately. Online providers control any data retained by them; contact them to exercise applicable privacy rights. Contact Operation Embedded for questions or requests concerning information you send us directly, such as support emails. Support messages are used to respond to and resolve your request.

Tracking and changes

Vibe PCB includes no advertising or developer analytics tracker. App Store purchase records and diagnostics are handled by Apple under its policies. Apple may share diagnostics with the developer according to your settings. We may update this policy when app behavior changes; the effective date identifies the current version.
