Vibe PCB Support

Working name: Vibe PCB may be renamed before release. These pages currently describe the app developed by Operation Embedded.

Contact Contact@operationembedded.net. Include the app version/build, device model, steps to reproduce and copied error text. Do not send API keys, account tokens or confidential designs.

Start without AI

Create a Pocket LED template, inspect its schematic and PCB, then tap Build. Open Board details to review DRC and Export to save a project or manufacturing ZIP to Files. No AI key or host is required for local CAD. Online AI requires your own provider connection and separate usage charges. Apple Basic AI is limited, read-only help on compatible devices.

Recover your work

Open Workspace settings → Automatic recovery copies. Choose a point and restore it as project copies. For independent backups, use Save workspace backup and save to Files. Automatic recovery points stay in app storage and do not survive app deletion. Restore Files backups with Restore projects & chat from backup.

Pencil and routing

In Pencil mode, fingers navigate while Pencil edits. Route traces by selecting an assigned pad, adding corners and finishing on the same net. Board details → Ground pours & power planes assigns one board-wide pour per layer. Build refills pours with native KiCad and checks preserved manual traces. Saved fills are invalidated after board edits; rebuild before export.

Supported scope

Local CAD supports 2/4/6/8 layers, 12–200 mm boards, up to 128 components and 1024 pads, top-side component placement, straight traces and through vias. Board-wide pours support thermal and solid pad connections. Controlled impedance, blind/buried vias, differential-pair tuning, electrical simulation and arbitrary polygon-zone editing are not supported. DRC checks geometry and connectivity, not circuit correctness.

Compatible KiCad boards and single-sheet schematics import as converted editable copies, subject to the import review’s restrictions. Other boards can be inspected read-only with native KiCad. Conversion is not lossless native KiCad editing. Native schematic round-trip export is not available. Preserve original files. Downloaded footprints require manufacturer verification of physical geometry and pin functions.

Before ordering, independently review exported Gerbers, drills, BOM, assembly rotations, pinouts, ratings and fabrication stack-up. Inventory is a source-time reference and does not reserve stock or place orders.
