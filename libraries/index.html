Modified KiCad footprint collection

Original contributors: KiCad library contributors. The 21-footprint modified collection remains under CC-BY-SA-4.0 with the KiCad design exception; it is separate from the app software license.

Vibe PCB converts KiCad 10.0.3 pad/body/courtyard geometry to JSON. Rounded SMD pads are conservatively represented as rectangles. Every entry retains its upstream source URL and SHA-256. No manufacturer STEP models are bundled.
